# 1. Read the Text

## My Name Is Murad

Hello!

My name is Murad. I am 19 years old.

I am from Azerbaijan. I live in Baku.

I am a university student. I study computer science.

I have a small family. I live with my parents.

I have two friends at university. Their names are Ali and Samir.

I like football and music.

I am happy to be a student.

---

# 2. Azərbaycan dilində mənası
