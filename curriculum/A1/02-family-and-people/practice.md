# ✍️ Practice

Bu məşqlərdə **my, your, his, her, our, their** formalarını möhkəmləndiririk.

---

## 1. Boşluğu doldur

Düzgün sözü yaz:

**my / your / his / her / our / their**

1. I have a book. It is ___ book.
2. You have a phone. It is ___ phone.
3. He has a brother. He is ___ brother.
4. She has a sister. She is ___ sister.
5. We have a house. It is ___ house.
6. They have a family. It is ___ family.

---

## 2. Düzgün variantı seç

1. My / I name is Ali.
2. She / Her phone is here.
3. He / His father is a teacher.
4. We / Our family is small.
5. They / Their house is big.
6. You / Your book is new.

---

## 3. Səhvi tap və düzəlt

1. My is book.
2. Her is name Anna.
3. His father is teacher.
4. Our is house small.
5. Their family are big.

---

## 4. Azərbaycan dilindən ingilis dilinə çevir

1. Mənim anam müəllimdir.
2. Mənim atam sürücüdür.
3. Mənim qardaşım tələbədir.
4. Onun adı Annadır.
5. Onun atası müəllimdir.
6. Bizim evimiz kiçikdir.
7. Onların ailəsi böyükdür.
8. Sənin telefonun buradadır.

---

## 5. Cümlə qur

Verilən sözlərdən düzgün cümlə qur:

1. my / is / This / book
2. name / My / Ali / is
3. your / What / name / is?
4. father / His / teacher / is / a
5. sister / Her / student / is / a
6. house / Our / small / is
7. family / Their / big / is

---

## 6. Öz cümlələrini yaz

Aşağıdakı sözlərin hər biri ilə bir cümlə yaz:

* my
* your
* his
* her
* our
* their

Məsələn:

**My book is new.**

---

## 7. Speaking Practice

Bu cümlələri ucadan de:

> My name is ______.

> My family is ______.

> My mother is ______.

> My father is ______.

> My friend is ______.

> Our house is ______.

> My phone is ______.

> My book is ______.

Sonra telefona baxmadan öz ailən haqqında ən azı **8 cümlə** danış.
